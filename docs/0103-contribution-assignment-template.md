## 6. Assignment fallback

If, despite valid execution, a specified economic right cannot legally be
transferred but can be exclusively licensed, the Contributor grants the Owner
an exclusive license to that right on the fields in Section 3, worldwide, for
its remaining statutory term, with authority to sublicense and to permit
modification and derivative use. The compensation arrangement in Section 4
also covers this fallback.

The grant is subject to Mandatory Law on form, duration, termination and
remuneration. It does not claim to cure a missing signature or another mandatory
form defect, obtain rights belonging to someone else or make a non-transferable
right transferable. If neither transfer nor this exclusive license is valid,
the parties must execute a suitable further instrument before production use.

## 8. Patent permissions and further assistance

For patent claims the Contributor owns or controls and may license, necessarily
infringed by the identified Contribution as delivered, the Contributor grants
the Owner a worldwide, royalty-free license for the applicable patent term to
make, have made, use, offer for sale, sell and import that Contribution and to
authorize recipients to do so. This permission is limited to claims actually
controlled and necessary for that Contribution, not unrelated inventions or
third-party patents.

No patent ownership or invention registration right is transferred by this
paragraph. Such transfers require separately identified rights and required
instruments. The Contributor will reasonably assist with confirmatory acts,
with reasonable documented external costs reimbursed unless otherwise agreed.
No automatic power of attorney is granted.
